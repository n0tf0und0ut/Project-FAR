# Research Index

Status: Research navigation; non-canonical

## Purpose

This file is the navigation entry point for exploratory and provisional work under `research/`.

## Authority boundary

The Repository Domain Registry defines `research/` as non-canonical exploratory/provisional material. Nothing indexed here becomes accepted theory, current governance, or claim authority merely by appearing in this directory or this index.

Use [`../docs/CANONICAL_MAP.md`](../docs/CANONICAL_MAP.md) to locate current canonical sources and [`../docs/governance/research-execution-charter.md`](../docs/governance/research-execution-charter.md) for research status and promotion rules.

## Major areas

- `axiomatization/` — exploratory axiomatization work.
- `bibliography/` and `literature/` — research-source records.
- `comparisons/` — exploratory comparative work.
- [`workflow-efficacy/README.md`](workflow-efficacy/README.md) — non-executed, exploratory 30-candidate paired trial of agent workflow instruction efficacy; no measured performance claim.
- [`uncertain-routing/README.md`](uncertain-routing/README.md) — research-only explicit-contract dispatch gate and separately scored under-specified agent-choice pilot; no general architecture promotion.
- `discovery/` — discovery records.
- `foundations/` — research concerning foundational questions; not the accepted `../foundations/` domain.
- `methodology/` — research-stage methodology work; not the canonical methodology/governance layer.
- `notes/` — working research notes.
- `open-problems/` — research-stage problem material.
- `proofs/` — proof research and draft proof-navigation material; not accepted proof authority unless separately promoted by canonical governance.
- `validation/` — research validation investigations and execution evidence.

## Preservation rule

Research records may preserve superseded, failed, incomplete, or contradictory work for provenance. Their presence is evidence of research history, not evidence that their claims are accepted or current. Current status and authority must be read from the canonical map and applicable governance/status registers.
