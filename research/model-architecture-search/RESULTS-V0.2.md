# FAR-MAS-MATCHED-002 — execution and negative result

**Status: Research / 80 of 80 scored runs completed / full original result preserved / primary falsification screen FAILED.**

This is an executed follow-up to the bounded architecture search in `PR #615`. It addresses the original v0.1 result's model-size confound without changing the v0.1 model source, registered protocol, outputs or evidence. ## Primary outcome (length 15, 120 steps)

The primary predeclared contrast was two attention blocks of width 16 against a single attention block of approximately the same parameter count (width 22):

- Stacked model: 4,834 parameters; **88.43%** mean held-out macro accuracy.
- Single wider model: 4,644 parameters; **87.00%** mean held-out macro accuracy.
- Mean paired contrast: **+1.427 percentage points**, below the required +2 points.
- Seed-by-seed paired differences (percentage points): **−8.80, −1.86, −3.91, +13.78, +7.92**. Only **2 of 5** seeds favored stacked attention; the gate required at least 4.
- Per-family mean contrasts: first-bit recall **0.00** points, endpoint XOR **+3.93** points, majority **+0.35** points. No family failed the >5-point-regression gate.

**Verdict: FAIL** (two of three necessary engineering criteria failed). This is a descriptive finite-corpus result, not a population significance test. It cannot support an established architectural improvement.

## Complete secondary comparisons

Values are mean held-out *macro accuracy*, averaged across the same five training seeds. Higher is better.

| Sequence length | Training steps | Two attention blocks | Single wide attention | Wide GRU | Wide causal convolution |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 15 | 120 | 88.43% | 87.00% | 82.66% | 55.00% |
| 15 | 240 | 96.40% | **99.86%** | 94.80% | 55.65% |
| 17 | 120 | 86.88% | **88.11%** | 80.89% | 54.32% |
| 17 | 240 | 96.11% | 93.86% | **98.18%** | 55.24% |

The ranking changes when sequence length or optimization budget changes. The test was steps-matched, not exactly FLOP- or elapsed-time-matched. The particularly poor convolution performance is unsurprising for a three-token causal receptive field when some outcomes depend on the first token; it is a recorded failure, not grounds to discard that preregistered arm.

The task labels are mathematically computable from the binary inputs without a neural network. Therefore neither near-perfect accuracy nor improved synthetic task learning constitutes a discovery of intelligence, broad reasoning ability, or a revolutionary architecture. A rule-based oracle achieves 100% by construction.
